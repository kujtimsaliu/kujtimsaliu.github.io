About

I'm a mobile engineer from Skopje who likes owning things end to end: architecture, UI, release pipelines, and everything in between. At Moonlight, an interactive storytelling platform with ~70K users, I'm the only mobile engineer: I led the move from our native iOS app to a single Flutter codebase and now own architecture, CI/CD, and releases on both stores. Before going full-time at Moonlight, I spent almost three years at Shortcut Balkans building production features for REMA 1000's grocery app, used by millions in Norway.

On the side I co-founded Automatik.mk, a price-comparison platform for the Macedonian market, and I'm finishing a Computer Engineering degree at FINKI. My day-to-day tools are Swift, SwiftUI and Flutter, backed by CI/CD with GitHub Actions and Fastlane.
